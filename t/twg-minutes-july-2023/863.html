TWG Minutes - July 2023

News Developer
peterfpeterson July 19, 2023, 12:57pm 1

The Technical Working Group (TWG) met this month (2023-07-18) to discuss various issues. An overview of the points covered during the meeting is as follows:

  • Review the dates for the v6.8 milestone
  • Progress updates on ILL and ISIS provide osx build servers
  • Discussion of dependency pinning
  • Discussion of mantid’s adherence to github’s community standards

The full minutes are located on github.
