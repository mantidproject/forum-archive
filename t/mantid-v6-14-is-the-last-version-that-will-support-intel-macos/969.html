Mantid v6.14 is the last version that will support Intel macOS

News
peterfpeterson August 20, 2025, 4:05pm 1

At the TWG meeting held on 2025-08-19, we decided mantid will drop support for intel osx after v6.14 is released. This is related to apple removing support for intel in future versions of their operating system and reducing the mantid projects maintenance burden.
