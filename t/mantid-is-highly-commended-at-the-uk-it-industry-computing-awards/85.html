Mantid is Highly Commended at the UK IT Industry Computing Awards

News User
NickDraper November 23, 2015, 2:42pm 1

The Mantid Project highly commended in the BCS & Computing UK IT Industry Awards 2015 in the Project Excellence category for the best not for profit project. This was a crowded category of ten strong candidates and while we were just beaten by the Cabinet Office, we were picked out as highly commended. A big thank you to everyone that helps to make this project the international success it is.

The results of all of the categories are available here:
