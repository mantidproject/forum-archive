Hi Malcolm,

I had suggested ConvertToPointData as the SNAP46756_monitors_ioio.csv file you previously sent me, loaded as PointData.

I think what you want to do is open MantidPlot > Preferences > 2D Plots and un-check the box beside “Normalize histogram to bin width”

Does this work for you?
